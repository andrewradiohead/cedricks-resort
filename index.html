A family-run resort on a quiet cove

Cedrick's Resort opened in 2012 as six bamboo-and-nipa cottages built by the Cedrick family on land they had fished from for three generations. Today it has 24 rooms, an open-air restaurant and a small dive shop, and it is still managed by the family.

We sit within reach of the marine reserves and white-sand beaches that make Puerto Galera a UNESCO Man and Biosphere Reserve. Guests choose us for calm water, honest prices and staff who know the coast by name.

Mission

Give every guest a restful, safe and memorable coastal stay while protecting the reef that sustains our community.

Vision

The most trusted family resort in Oriental Mindoro, known for sincere hospitality and responsible tourism.

Location. Sabang–Balatero coastal road, Puerto Galera, Oriental Mindoro (fictional address for academic use).

Target market. Couples, families, small barkadas, and certified or beginner divers from Metro Manila, Batangas and abroad.

What sets us apart. Sea-view rooms at mid-range prices, in-house dive shop, local-staffed island tours and a refill-only plastic policy.

Tourism value. We source seafood and produce from nearby fisherfolk and farms, and about 90% of our team lives within 5 km of the resort.

Travel guide

Everything worth knowing before you pack.

Best time to visit

November to May is dry with calm seas. June to October is wetter and rougher; boat tours may be cancelled for safety.

What to bring

  • Reef-safe sunscreen
  • Swimwear, light cotton clothes, a light jacket
  • Refillable bottle, cash for local shops

Estimated budget

About ₱3,500 to ₱6,000 per person per day, including room share, meals and one activity.

Safety

  • Swim only in marked areas
  • Wear life vests on all boat trips
  • Do not dive within 18 hours of flying

Local customs and etiquette

Greet staff and villagers with a smile and "po" or "opo". Ask before photographing people. Cover up when leaving the beach area.

Environment

Do not touch or stand on coral. Take trash back to shore. We use refill stations, not single-use bottles.

Accessibility

Garden Cottages and the restaurant have step-free paths. Tell us your needs and we will prepare a ground-floor room.

Emergency information

Resort front desk: +63 917 555 0142 (24 hours). Emergency hotline: 911. Nearest health center: Puerto Galera Municipal Health Office.

Dress code

Beachwear is fine on the sand. Smart casual is appreciated at dinner. Shirts are required in the restaurant.
